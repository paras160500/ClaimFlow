Investigation Note -- Claim CL001545

Claim CL001545 was reviewed because the claimed repair amount was significantly high relative to the vehicle's assessed value. The investigator requested an independent repair estimate for comparison.
Claim CL001545 was filed only 29 days after the customer's previous claim. The investigator confirmed the two incidents were unrelated based on the incident descriptions and repair shop records.
The repair shop associated with claim CL001545 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
