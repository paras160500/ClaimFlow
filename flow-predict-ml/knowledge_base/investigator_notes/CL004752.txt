Investigation Note -- Claim CL004752

Claim CL004752 was reviewed because the claimed repair amount was significantly high relative to the vehicle's assessed value. The investigator requested an independent repair estimate for comparison.
The customer on claim CL004752 had 3 previous claims on file. The investigator cross-referenced the claim history and found the pattern consistent with the customer's usage profile.
The repair shop associated with claim CL004752 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
Additional documents were requested from the claimant before a decision could be made.
