Investigation Note -- Claim CL015233

Claim CL015233 was reviewed because the claimed repair amount was significantly high relative to the vehicle's assessed value. The investigator requested an independent repair estimate for comparison.
The repair shop associated with claim CL015233 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
Additional documents were requested from the claimant before a decision could be made.
