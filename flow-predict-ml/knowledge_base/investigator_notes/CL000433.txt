Investigation Note -- Claim CL000433

Claim CL000433 was reviewed because the claimed repair amount was significantly high relative to the vehicle's assessed value. The investigator requested an independent repair estimate for comparison.
The customer on claim CL000433 had 10 previous claims on file. The investigator cross-referenced the claim history and found the pattern consistent with the customer's usage profile.
Claim CL000433 was filed only 1 days after the customer's previous claim. The investigator confirmed the two incidents were unrelated based on the incident descriptions and repair shop records.
The repair shop associated with claim CL000433 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
The claim was escalated for further investigation.
