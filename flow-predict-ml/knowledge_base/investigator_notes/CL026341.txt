Investigation Note -- Claim CL026341

The policy on claim CL026341 was only 14 days old when the claim was filed. The investigator requested the original policy application and confirmed there was no prior coverage gap before proceeding.
Claim CL026341 was reviewed because the claimed repair amount was significantly high relative to the vehicle's assessed value. The investigator requested an independent repair estimate for comparison.
The customer on claim CL026341 had 12 previous claims on file. The investigator cross-referenced the claim history and found the pattern consistent with the customer's usage profile.
The repair shop associated with claim CL026341 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
The claim was rejected following the investigation.
