Investigation Note -- Claim CL024060

The customer on claim CL024060 had 7 previous claims on file. The investigator cross-referenced the claim history and found the pattern consistent with the customer's usage profile.
Claim CL024060 was filed only 26 days after the customer's previous claim. The investigator confirmed the two incidents were unrelated based on the incident descriptions and repair shop records.
The repair shop associated with claim CL024060 has an elevated historical investigation rate. The investigator requested the original repair invoice directly from the shop rather than relying on the submitted estimate alone.
The claim was ultimately approved after review.
